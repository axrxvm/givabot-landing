## 📢 Announcement: Giva Bot Retirement

_After much thought and reflection, I’ve decided to officially retire **Giva Bot**._

Over time, its role has naturally diminished as the needs of our community have evolved. While it’s been a valuable tool and served many of you well, _continuing to maintain it is no longer sustainable given current priorities and the direction I’m moving toward_.

That said, this isn’t a goodbye — just the closing of one chapter. While I may or may not return to building Discord-related tools in the future, I’m exploring new ideas and projects that I’m genuinely excited about. There’s more to come, and I look forward to sharing those updates when the time is right.

I want to thank everyone who used Giva Bot, provided feedback, and helped shape it into what it became. Your support has meant a lot, and it’s been a rewarding experience building something that made an impact — no matter how big or small. It's been an incredible journey for us along with the Bot from when it started in 2021. It was my very first big project that I can never forget.

---

**Goodbye Giva Bot**  
~ Aarav
